1. Prime Selection

RSA starts with two different prime numbers, traditionally called p and q. The grid below is the Sieve of Eratosthenes: every number from 2 up to the limit is checked, and any number that is a multiple of a smaller prime is crossed out (a "composite"). What's left uncrossed is prime.

Capped at 2000 so the grid renders instantly and stays readable on a projector. (The assignment's outer safety limit is 10⁶ — see the report.)
